## 💡 Why Sponsor Apertur?

Most modern screen recording and screenshot tools require expensive monthly subscriptions ($15 to $29 per month), impose strict 5-minute recording limits on free accounts, or upload private recordings and confidential browser tabs to third-party cloud servers.

**Apertur was created to provide a completely free, local, and professional alternative:**

- **Zero Cloud Surveillance**: 100% of video recordings and screenshots stay on your personal device inside local IndexedDB storage.
- **No Artificial Paywalls**: Unlimited recording duration, 1080p 60 FPS video, lossless full-page screenshots, and built-in trimming tools are completely free for everyone.
- **MIT Licensed**: Free for personal, educational, developer, and commercial use with zero vendor lock-in.

Building and maintaining high-performance browser extensions, keeping up with rapid Google Chrome Manifest V3 API updates, optimizing hardware video encoding, and delivering lossless screenshot capabilities requires significant time and focus. 

By sponsoring creator **[Tahsin Soyak](https://github.com/tahsinsoyak)** on Patreon, you ensure that Apertur remains independent, actively developed, and accessible to users worldwide.

## 🎯 What Your Sponsorship Funds

Your contributions directly support the following initiatives:

1. **Chrome Web Store Distribution**:
   - Funding developer account maintenance, store verification, and automated build pipelines.
2. **Advanced Codec & Performance Engineering**:
   - Researching WebCodecs API integrations, AV1 hardware encoding, and 120 FPS high-refresh capture for modern displays.
3. **Upcoming Feature Roadmap**:
   - Circular webcam Picture-in-Picture (PiP) video overlays.
   - Animated GIF and MP4 export options.
   - Microphone noise suppression and dynamic audio level meters.
   - Advanced screenshot canvas annotations (arrows, text callouts, redaction blur).
4. **Documentation & User Support**:
   - Writing comprehensive guides, answering community questions on GitHub, and maintaining regression test suites.
