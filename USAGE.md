## ⚡ 60-Second Quick Start

1. **Open Apertur**: Click the Apertur icon in your Chrome toolbar.
2. **Choose Recording Mode**:
   - **Browser Window** (*Recommended*): Captures your browser smoothly across logins, redirects, and all tabs.
   - **Entire Screen**: Captures your monitor, other applications, and code editors.
   - **This Tab Only**: 1-click active tab capture.
3. **Configure Audio**:
   - Toggle **Tab Sound** to capture system/website audio.
   - Toggle **Microphone** to open the audio drawer: select your microphone, check the live VU meter, and adjust boost gain.
4. **Choose Visual Helpers**:
   - Turn on **Highlight mouse & clicks** and pick your favorite color (`Amber`, `Blue`, `Purple`, `Red`, `Green`).
   - Leave **3s Countdown** enabled to get ready before recording begins.
5. **Start Recording**: Click **Start Recording**, select your window/screen in the Chrome prompt, and begin!
6. **Finish**: Click **Finish & Save Video** on the floating on-screen HUD or popup.

## 📸 Instant Screenshots & Annotations

| Action | Global Shortcut | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Area Crop (Snip)** | <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> | Drag crosshair to crop any region. Copy to clipboard or save PNG. |
| **Full Page Stitch** | <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> | Auto-scrolls and stitches entire tall webpage into a lossless PNG. |
| **Visible Viewport** | <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>V</kbd> | Instant snapshot of currently visible view. |

### Annotation Studio:
Open any screenshot in your **Media Library** -> Click **Annotate**:
- ✏️ **Pen**: Freehand markup
- ↗️ **Arrow**: Directional bug/feature callouts
- 🔲 **Box**: Rectangle frames
- 🟡 **Highlight**: Semi-transparent marker
- 🔤 **Text**: Click to type high-contrast text badges
- ↩️ **Undo**, **Clear**, and **Save to Library**

## 🎬 Video Player Studio & Export Presets

When recording ends:
- **Trim Sliders**: Move **Start** and **End** sliders to cut out dead time, click **Preview Cut**, then **Save Trimmed Video**.
- **Click-to-Rename**: Click the title to rename your recording inline.
- **Export Presets** (Caret next to Download):
  - **Master 1080p WebM**: Full 60 FPS VP9 master.
  - **Web-Ready 720p**: Compact video under 25MB for Slack & email.
  - **Audio Only**: Extracted voice/soundtrack WebM (Opus).
  - **Short Animated Loop**: 5s preview clip for documentation.
