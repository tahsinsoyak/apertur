FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about Apertur.

Everything you need to know about recording, trimming, screenshots, privacy, and open-source usage.

Which recording mode should I choose: Browser Window, Entire Screen, or This Tab Only?

We recommend Browser Window for almost all web recordings. Unlike single-tab capture, Browser Window mode continues recording smoothly when you click links, log in via OAuth, or navigate across different pages, and it automatically keeps your Pause/Finish bar visible.

How do I trim the start or end of a recording and rename the file?

When your recording finishes and opens in the Video Player Studio, use the Trim Start and Trim End sliders below the player to select the exact range you want to keep, then click Save Trimmed Copy. To rename any video, simply click the title or the pencil icon at the top of the player and press Enter. Your custom name is saved to IndexedDB and used automatically when downloading the .webm file.

How do the 3-way PNG screenshot tools work?

You can capture lossless PNG screenshots in three places: (1) click the camera icon in the top-right header of the extension popup to snap the active tab anytime, (2) click the camera button on the floating in-page control bar while recording a video, or (3) click Frame PNG inside the Video Player Studio to save the exact video frame currently on screen.

Where are my recorded videos saved? Are they uploaded to the cloud?

Your recordings are never uploaded to any external server. Every video is stored 100% locally inside your browser's private IndexedDB storage on your computer. You can watch them anytime in My Saved Videos or download them as standard .webm video files.

Can I record both website audio and my microphone voice at the same time?

Yes. Turn on both Tab Sound and Microphone in the Apertur popup before starting. Apertur mixes both audio streams in real time at studio-grade 192 kbps.
