FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about Apertur.

Everything you need to know about recording, privacy, and open-source usage.

Which recording mode should I choose: Browser Window, Entire Screen, or This Tab Only?

We recommend Browser Window for almost all web recordings. Unlike single-tab capture, Browser Window mode continues recording smoothly when you click links, log in via OAuth, or navigate across different pages, and it automatically keeps your Pause/Finish bar visible.

Where are my recorded videos saved? Are they uploaded to the cloud?

Your recordings are never uploaded to any external server. Every video is stored 100% locally inside your browser's private IndexedDB storage on your computer. You can watch them anytime in My Saved Videos or download them as standard .webm video files.

Is Apertur really 100% free with no watermarks or time limits?

Yes! Apertur is released under the open-source MIT License. There are no watermarks, no 5-minute recording caps, no account sign-ups, and no paid tiers.

Can I record both website audio and my microphone voice at the same time?

Yes. Turn on both Tab Sound and Microphone in the Apertur popup before starting. Apertur mixes both audio streams in real time at studio-grade 192 kbps.
