FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about sponsorship.

Everything you need to know about becoming a sponsor on Patreon.

Will Apertur ever charge users or lock features behind a paywall?

No. Apertur is released under the permissive MIT license and will always remain 100% free. Every feature, including 1080p 60 FPS recording, unlimited duration, built-in trimming, and lossless screenshots, is accessible to everyone at zero cost.

How do I claim my recognition after becoming a patron?

After joining on Patreon, your Patreon username (or company name and link of your choice) is reviewed and added to the official GitHub README and website sponsor wall within 48 hours. You can also message Tahsin Soyak directly on Patreon with your preferred name or logo.

Can I cancel or change my Patreon contribution at any time?

Yes. You have complete control over your Patreon membership. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your pledge at any time with no questions asked.

Do you accept corporate or one-time sponsorships?

Yes. If your company would like to make a custom annual contribution, request invoice billing, or prioritize specific enterprise features, please contact Tahsin Soyak on GitHub or through Patreon messages.
